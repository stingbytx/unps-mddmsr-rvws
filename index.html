How these reviews are collected

Every review on this page came from a customer conversation — during onboarding, over WhatsApp support, or in a follow-up call after a shop had been running on UniPOS for a few months. We publish what customers tell us in their own words.

  • Collected directly. Reviews are gathered by our team from the businesses using the software, not bought or aggregated from third-party platforms.
  • Lightly edited only. We fix spelling and trim for length, but we do not change what a reviewer said about the product.
  • Ratings are the reviewer's own. The star rating shown with each review reflects the score the business gave UniPOS.
  • No incentives. Customers are not paid, discounted or entered into a draw in exchange for a review.
  • Corrections welcome. If you are quoted here and something is wrong or outdated, message us and we will correct or remove it.
